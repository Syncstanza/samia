# Credits

- Quran text: Tanzil.net. Translation: Saheeh International.
- Page view (Madinah mushaf, 15 lines per page): page, line and word layout from the quran.ws/text Hafs dataset, licensed CC BY 4.0. Text and the UthmanicHafs font are from the King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Quran. Rebuild with `node tools/build-madani.mjs <path to @quran.ws/text/data>`.

## Tajweed colours

Tajweed rule positions come from [cpfair/quran-tajweed](https://github.com/cpfair/quran-tajweed) (CC BY 4.0), applied to the Tanzil text and mapped onto the mushaf page spelling by tools/build-tajweed.mjs.

## Listen (recitation audio)

Recordings and word timings are streamed from the [Quran.com](https://quran.com) content API (Quran Foundation); the backup source is [everyayah.com](https://everyayah.com). The audio belongs to the reciters and those services and is streamed, never stored or redistributed by Sami'a.
